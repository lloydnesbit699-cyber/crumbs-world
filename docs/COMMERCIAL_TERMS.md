## Tile-art licensing (this matters — read it)

Art packs carry their own licenses, independent of your Crumbs edition:

- **Starter pack** — original art made for Crumbs. Safe to use in
  commercial projects on Creator and Pro.
- **Utumno master sheet** — Project Utumno, released CC0 (public
  domain). Use it anywhere, commercially or not. A credit line is
  appreciated, not required.
- **Art you import** — your responsibility. If you slice a sprite sheet
  you don't own, that's between you and its owner. Crumbs can't launder
  a license.
- **InvSprite pack** — the Minecraft inventory sprites are
  Mojang/Microsoft art. The sliced pack is provided for your **personal
  use only** and is **never licensed for redistribution** — don't ship
  it in a public repo, a paid bundle, or a published game.

If you publish a game made in Crumbs, audit which art is on each map.
The editor will eventually flag pack provenance per tile; until then,
keep track yourself.
