# Crumbs HUD — Who It's For

## What it is

Crumbs HUD is a touch-first tile map painter with a tiny playable game
engine inside it. It runs on your phone — one Python script, one page in
your browser. You paint a dungeon with your finger, hit Play, and walk
through it ten seconds later. No installs beyond a terminal app, no
accounts, no cloud, no build step. Everything saves on the device.

## Who it's for

**Solo devs.** You have a game in your head and a phone in your pocket.
Crumbs lets you sketch real, walkable levels anywhere — the couch, the
bus, a lunch break — and playtest them immediately. Paint, play, fix,
repeat. When a map is good, export it and take it into your real engine.

**Classroom use.** Teaching game design, not toolchain wrangling. No
accounts to provision, no software to install on lab machines, nothing
in the cloud. Students open a page, paint a level, and play each other's
work the same period. The mission workshop gives structured assignments
for free: build a level around a goal, playtest it, iterate.

**Game-jam creators.** The jam starts Friday and your idea needs a map
by Saturday morning. Crumbs gets you from blank grid to a playtested
dungeon with objectives, items, NPCs, and a shop in an evening — on the
device already in your hand.

## What it isn't

- Not a Unity/Godot replacement. It's the sketchbook before the engine,
  and the playtester that fits in your pocket.
- Not an asset store. It ships a curated starter pack and a public-domain
  master sheet; the art pipeline is for importing *your* sheets.
- Not cloud SaaS. Your maps live on your device. Sharing is a local
  network feature, not a subscription.
- Not a coding course. You never have to see code — but it's all there,
  one readable Python file, if you want to.

## The pitch in one line

Paint a dungeon on your phone. Walk through it ten seconds later.
